Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information shOk collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account data: name, email, phone number, and optional profile photo.
Order data: delivery addresses, order contents, chat messages with riders and support, order history and ratings.
Location data: your device location when you grant permission (used for delivery addresses and nearby vendors), and rider location while a delivery is active (used for live tracking).
Device and usage data: push-notification tokens, device model and OS, and how you interact with the app.
We do not collect contact lists, call logs or other unrelated personal data.

2. How We Use It

We use your information to: take and deliver orders; match riders to requests; show order status and live tracking; process payments; send notifications you have enabled; provide customer support; prevent fraud and abuse; keep the Service secure; and improve the app. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing

We share only what is needed to operate the Service: your name, address and order details go to the vendor and rider handling your order; your chat messages go to the other party in that conversation; a rider on an active delivery shares their live location with the customer. Service providers that process data on our behalf include cloud image hosting (Cloudinary) and map/location providers. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer. We do not share data with advertisers.

4. Location

Customers: location is used to set delivery addresses and estimate delivery times; you can deny the permission and enter addresses manually, though some features may not work.
Riders: location is shared with the customer and the platform while a delivery is in progress so orders can be tracked; it is not continuously collected when you are off duty.

5. Notifications

We send order updates and, if enabled, promotions and offers. You can turn each category off in Settings > Notifications or at the system level; transactional updates required to deliver your order may still be sent while notifications are enabled for the app.

6. Automated Decisions

Some features use automated processing. For example matching a rider to your order, estimating delivery times, and flagging potentially fraudulent activity. These decisions affect service efficiency, and flags are reviewed before account action is taken. You may contest automated decisions through support.

7. Retention and Deletion

We keep your information while your account is active and for a reasonable period after, as needed for order records, dispute resolution, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Chat and order records may be retained in anonymized or aggregated form. You can request account deletion through Contact Us. We will delete or anonymize personal data that is no longer legally required.

8. Your Rights

You can view and correct your profile and addresses in the app at any time. Depending on applicable law, you may also have the right to access your data, request a copy, object to certain processing, restrict use, or request deletion. Contact us through the app to exercise any of these rights. We respond within a reasonable timeframe.

9. International Transfers

Some service providers may process or store data on servers outside your country. Where that happens, we rely on the providers' own safeguards, and your data remains subject to this Policy.

10. Security

We use encrypted transport, access controls and limited retention to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Notify us immediately if you suspect unauthorized use of your account. If a data breach occurs that affects your personal information, we will notify you and relevant authorities as required by law.

11. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

12. Third-Party Links and SDKs

The app may contain links or embedded services operated by third parties (for example map providers or a store listing). Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, which we encourage you to read.

13. Changes and Contact

We may update this Policy and will post changes in the app with a new date; continued use after changes means acceptance. For privacy questions or requests, use the Contact Us section in the app.
