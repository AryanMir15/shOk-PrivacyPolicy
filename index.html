Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information shOk collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have. It applies to the shOk app and every role inside it: customer, rider and vendor. By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account data: name, email address, phone number, password (stored only as a salted hash), optional profile photo, and the sign-in method you use, including a Google account identifier if you sign in with Google.
Order data: delivery addresses and their map coordinates, the contact name and phone number you attach to an address, order contents, order value, payment method and payment status, order history and ratings.
Chat and support data: messages you exchange with riders, vendors and support inside the app, plus files you attach to them.
Location data: your device location when you grant permission, used to set delivery addresses and find nearby shops. For riders, location while a delivery is active and while you are on duty, used for live tracking and dispatch.
Rider verification data: for rider accounts, the identity documents you submit for review (legal name, national identity card number, licence number, vehicle number, and photos of the documents and yourself). This data is not collected from customers or vendors.
Device and usage data: push notification tokens, device model, operating system, app version, and how you interact with the app.
Content you create: reviews, ratings, reported conversations and support tickets.

We do not collect your contact list, call logs, SMS messages, calendar, or other data unrelated to operating the Service.

2. How We Use It

We use your information to: create and secure your account; take, route and deliver orders; match riders to requests; show order status and live tracking; process payments and rider remittances; send the notifications you have enabled; provide customer support; review rider applications; prevent fraud, abuse and promotion misuse; keep the Service secure; diagnose crashes and fix bugs; and understand which features are used so we can improve them.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

3. Sharing

We share only what is needed to run the Service:

  • With vendors and riders: your name, delivery address, contact number, order details and the instructions you add, so the order can be prepared and delivered. Riders see the customer address for the delivery they accepted.
  • With the other party in a chat: the messages and attachments you send in that conversation.
  • With customers, for riders: a rider's approximate live location while a delivery is in progress.
  • With service providers that process data on our behalf: cloud image hosting (Cloudinary) for uploaded images, Google Maps for addresses, distance and routes, Google Firebase for sign-in verification and push notification delivery, and our database and hosting providers.
  • When required by law: to comply with a legal obligation, enforce our Terms, or protect the rights, safety and property of shOk, our users or the public.
  • In a business transfer: if the Service or its assets are transferred, user data may transfer as part of that transaction under this Policy.

4. Location

Customers: location is used to set delivery addresses, estimate distances and delivery times, and show nearby shops. You can deny the permission and enter addresses manually, although delivery estimates and nearby results will be less accurate.
Riders: location is collected while you are on duty and during a delivery so customers can track the order and dispatch can offer work. It is not continuously collected while you are off duty.

5. Notifications

We send order status updates and, if enabled, promotions and offers. You can switch each category off in Settings, or block notifications at the system level. Notification delivery requires a push token issued by Google Firebase, which is stored against your account until you sign out, delete the account, or the token is replaced by your device.

6. Automated Decisions

Some features use automated processing: matching a rider to an order, estimating delivery times, and flagging possibly fraudulent activity. These decisions affect how quickly a service is provided. Where a flag could lead to account action, it is reviewed by a person before that action is taken, and you may contest it by contacting support.

7. Retention and Account Deletion

You can delete your account yourself, inside the app. Open Settings and use the Delete Account option. You can also request deletion from the page at Delete Account or by emailing us. Deletion is immediate, not scheduled for later.

When you delete your account we remove or clear:

  • your name, email address, phone number, password and Google account identifier
  • your profile photo and any pending profile photo
  • every saved delivery address, including its map coordinates
  • your push notification tokens, so no further notifications reach your device
  • your favourites, and your rider verification documents if you applied to ride
  • the name, phone number, address text and coordinates attached to your past orders

We keep a limited record of completed transactions after deletion: order numbers, items, amounts, dates, ratings you left, and rider remittance records. We keep these because we need them for accounting, tax, refund disputes, rider payouts and fraud prevention, and because the vendor and rider on the other side of the order also hold that history. These records no longer identify you: the account they point to contains no name, contact details or address. We do not keep your data in a form that can be linked back to you for marketing, and we never re-use it to contact you.

Before you can delete: an order that is still in progress, a shop registered to your account, or an unpaid cash remittance on a rider account must be settled first. This protects the person waiting for the delivery, the customers of that shop, and money that is owed. The app tells you exactly what is blocking deletion, and support can help resolve it.

8. Your Rights

You can view and correct your name, phone number, email, photo and saved addresses in the app at any time, and you can change your password from Settings. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request a copy of your data, object to or restrict certain processing, or ask us to correct or delete specific information. Use the Contact section below and we will respond within a reasonable period, normally within 30 days.

9. International Transfers

Some service providers may store or process data on servers outside Pakistan. Where that happens, we rely on those providers' own safeguards and contractual commitments, and your data remains protected by this Policy.

10. Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Passwords are stored as salted hashes and are never readable by us. Access to production data is limited to the accounts that need it to operate the Service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please use a unique password and tell us immediately if you suspect someone else has access to your account. If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

11. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

12. Third-Party Links and Services

The app may contain links to, or rely on, services operated by third parties, such as map providers or app stores. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, which we encourage you to read.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy as the Service changes. When we do, we post the new version here with a new date, and where the change is significant we tell you in the app. Continuing to use the Service after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
