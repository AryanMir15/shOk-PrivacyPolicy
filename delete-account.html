Delete Your shOk Account

Last updated: 4 November 2026

You can delete your shOk account at any time. You do not need to contact support, and deletion happens immediately once you confirm. This page explains how to do it, what is removed, and what we keep for accounting and dispute records.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the shOk app and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Find the Danger Zone section at the bottom.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Read the confirmation, type DELETE when asked, and confirm with your password.

Your account is deleted the moment the last step completes. You are signed out and returned to the login screen.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot sign in, email us and we will delete the account for you:

To: shok.app.official@gmail.com

Subject: Delete my shOk account

Include: the email address or phone number on the account. If you have one, include the account name so we can find it faster.

For your protection we may ask you to confirm ownership of the account email or phone number before proceeding. Requests are actioned within 7 days, and we reply once the account is gone.

Before you delete

Deletion cannot be undone. Your order history and saved addresses disappear from your account, your ratings and reviews stay attached to an anonymous account, and you will need to register again to place new orders. If someone else still owes you a delivery or a refund, wait until it is settled.

Some situations block deletion until they are resolved, because other people or money are involved:

  • an order you placed is still in progress (pending, accepted, preparing, ready or picked up)
  • you are currently delivering an order as a rider
  • your account owns a vendor shop, which would otherwise be left without an owner
  • you are a rider with an unpaid cash remittance balance

The app names the exact blocker and support can help you clear it. Once cleared, deletion goes through normally.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Removed from your account Kept, without your identity
Name, email address, phone number, password, Google sign-in link Order number, items ordered, amounts, dates
Profile photo and any photo pending review Payment method and payment status per order
All saved delivery addresses and their map coordinates Ratings and reviews you wrote, shown without your name
Push notification tokens, so no notifications reach the device Rider remittance and payout records, for money owed and paid
Favourites, and rider verification documents if you applied to ride Delivery address text, contact name and contact phone on past orders

We keep the right-hand column because accounting, tax, refund disputes, rider payouts and fraud prevention require it, and because the vendor and rider on the other side of each order also hold that history. Those records point to an account with no name, email, phone number or address, so they cannot be linked back to you, and we never use them to contact you. The full detail is in our Privacy Policy, section 7.

Other data you may want removed

If you have sent support tickets from a different email address, or you believe other information about you exists outside the account, mention it in your deletion request and we will look into it.
